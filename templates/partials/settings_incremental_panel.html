Incremental backups

VMware Changed Block Tracking (CBT) stores deltas after an initial full backup. Requires local or NFS storage — not S3.

When disabled, every backup is a full VM copy to a date folder.

Full backup interval After this many incremental backups, NovaBak creates a synthetic full and trims the chain.

Default: 7 incrementals before a new full base.

S3 storage is selected — incremental backups are not available. NovaBak will use full backups only.
